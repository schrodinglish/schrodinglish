# 🖥️ Tech News — April 30, 2026

## 💰 Big Tech Earnings: $665B AI Bet

Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft all reported earnings yesterday, collectively estimating they will invest up to **$665 billion** in AI this year — nearly 75% more than the $381 billion they spent in 2025.

- **Alphabet** was the standout: its shares climbed as much as 6% in after-hours trading after reporting earnings and revenue that beat analyst expectations, while also raising its AI infrastructure spending estimate.
- **Meta** struggled: shares tumbled 9% after the company hiked its full-year capital expenditure guidance to a range of $125–$145 billion, overshadowing a better-than-expected Q1 report.
- **Amazon** surged: the stock climbed more than 2% on the back of a blockbuster first-quarter report.
- **Microsoft** dipped: shares fell about 2%.

## 🤖 OpenAI Misses Targets, Rattles Market

According to the Wall Street Journal, OpenAI saw both its Q1 revenue and user growth miss expectations — the company aimed for 1 billion weekly active users but reached approximately 900 million. The company's overall monthly revenue has also fallen short of internal targets several times, signaling that the AI application layer is hitting bottlenecks in scaling.
