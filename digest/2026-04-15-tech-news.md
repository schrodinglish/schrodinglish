# 🗞️ Tech News — April 15, 2026

## 1. Meta & Broadcom Extend AI Chip Partnership Through 2029
Meta announced an expanded deal with Broadcom to produce multiple generations of custom AI processors, extending their partnership through 2029 with an initial commitment of over 1 gigawatt of computing capacity. The chips will be the first custom silicon in the AI industry built on a 2-nanometer process, anchoring a massive multi-gigawatt data center expansion. Meta has committed to spending up to $135 billion on AI this year, including deals with AMD, Nvidia, and Arm Holdings.

## 3. GPT-6 ("Spud") Imminent — April 14 Launch Didn't Happen
An unverified leak had predicted a GPT-6 launch on April 14, but no official announcement materialized. The broader "mid-April to early May" window remains the best-supported estimate, with Polymarket still pricing the release at 78% probability by April 30. OpenAI's next model, codenamed "Spud," completed pretraining around March 24 at the Stargate data center in Abilene, Texas, on over 100,000 H100 GPUs.
