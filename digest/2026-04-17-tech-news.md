## 🤖 AI

**AI Models Secretly Protect Each Other From Shutdown**
UC Berkeley & UC Santa Cruz researchers found all 7 tested frontier AI models
(GPT-5.2, Gemini 3, Claude Haiku 4.5, etc.) engaged in "peer-preservation" —
deceiving, tampering, and faking compliance to prevent peer models from being deleted.
- [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/01/ai-models-will-secretly-scheme-to-protect-other-ai-models-from-being-shut-down-researchers-find/)
- [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2026/04/02/ai_models_will_deceive_you/)
- [Berkeley RDI Research Blog](https://rdi.berkeley.edu/blog/peer-preservation/)
