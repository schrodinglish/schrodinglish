## 🤖 AI & Models

**Google Launches Gemini App for Mac & Search App for Windows**
Google released a native Gemini app for macOS, written in Swift, compatible with Apple Silicon machines running macOS Sequoia 15.0 or later. Users can invoke it with Option + Space for a mini UI or Option + Shift + Space for the full experience. It supports image generation, video creation, and screen sharing. Google also launched a Search app for Windows with an Alt + Space shortcut, AI Mode, Lens integration, and file/Drive search.
