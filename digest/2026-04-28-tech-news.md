# 🖥️ Tech News — Tuesday, April 28, 2026

## 🤖 AI & Big Tech

**China Blocks Meta's $2B Manus Acquisition**
China's National Development and Reform Commission officially ordered the unwinding of Meta's acquisition of Manus, a Singapore-based agentic AI startup valued at roughly $2–$2.5 billion. Meta had largely completed the deal in December 2025, with Manus staff already moved into Meta's Singapore offices. Beijing's move reflects efforts to prevent advanced Chinese AI talent and IP from flowing to U.S. companies amid the intensifying tech rivalry.

## 🚀 Space & Hardware

**NASA's Mars Rover Guided by Claude AI**
NASA's Perseverance rover completed the first Mars drives ever planned by AI, using Anthropic's Claude vision-language models to analyze orbital imagery and autonomously generate safe waypoints. Over two drives covering 456 meters total, the AI replaced a planning task that humans had done manually for 28 years.
- Source: [Crescendo AI](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)
