# 🖥️ Tech News — April 22, 2026

## 1. Apple CEO Transition: Tim Cook Out, John Ternus In
Apple announced Tim Cook will step down as CEO on September 1, handing the reins to hardware chief **John Ternus**, who will become executive chairman. Ternus, who has led hardware engineering since 2021, signals Apple's bet on tighter chip-device integration in the AI era. Apple also reorganized its hardware group into five units under new Chief Hardware Officer **Johny Srouji** — covering silicon, advanced technologies, platform architecture, hardware engineering, and project management.

## 2. Amazon Doubles Down on Anthropic: $5B Now, $20B More Possible
Amazon is investing **$5 billion immediately** in Anthropic, with up to $20 billion more tied to milestones — bringing total commitments to over **$33 billion**. In return, Anthropic committed to spending more than **$100 billion on AWS** over the next decade, including use of Amazon's Trainium2/3 chips and up to 5 gigawatts of AI capacity.

## 3. Google in Talks with Marvell to Build New AI Inference Chips
Google is reportedly co-developing two chips with **Marvell Technology**: a memory processing unit and a new inference-optimized TPU. The move diversifies Google beyond Broadcom and signals a broader push into custom silicon as the AI market shifts from training to inference workloads.

## 5. AI Privacy Crackdown: Clarifai Deletes 3M OkCupid Photos
**Clarifai** deleted 3 million OkCupid user photos and the facial-recognition models trained on them, following FTC scrutiny over deceptive data-sharing practices. The photos dated back to 2014 and were shared without adequate user disclosure — a warning shot for the industry's legacy training data practices.

🔗 [Clarifai/OkCupid Privacy Case — Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/)
